Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 23, 2026

Welcome to ORVEXA, developed by VEXLUME STUDIOS. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the ORVEXA application.

1. Information We Collect

ORVEXA does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use its main features. The app may store app-related information on your device, such as preferences, settings, or other information needed for app functionality.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

ORVEXA uses browser storage technologies, including Local Storage, to support certain features and remember information between sessions. This information is intended to remain in the app's local storage on your device. Clearing the app's data or storage may remove saved information.

3. Profile Images and Selected Files

Some features may allow you to select an image or file from your device. The app may use browser-based file reading functionality to process selected files. In the reviewed app version, no file-upload service or server-side file transfer was identified. Avoid selecting files containing sensitive information unless necessary.

4. Clipboard Access

Certain features may use clipboard functionality when you choose an action such as copying text. Clipboard access is intended to support the feature you requested.

5. Third-Party Services

ORVEXA uses Google Fonts to display its typography. When the app loads these fonts, your device may connect to Google-operated servers. These services may process technical information such as IP address and browser or device information according to their own policies.

For more information, review the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Data Sharing

In the reviewed version, no app feature was identified that sends personal information to an ORVEXA-operated server or shares user data with advertisers. Third-party services may process information as described in their own privacy policies.

7. Data Security

We aim to keep the app simple and privacy-conscious. However, no electronic storage or internet transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your device secure and avoid entering sensitive personal information into the app.

8. Children's Privacy

ORVEXA is a general entertainment application. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact the developer through the official app distribution listing.

9. Your Choices

You can stop using the app at any time. You may clear the app's local storage or app data through your device settings. This may delete locally saved preferences and other app information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features, technologies, or privacy practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised update date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or ORVEXA's privacy practices, contact VEXLUME STUDIOS through the official app distribution listing where ORVEXA is published.

Our Commitment
ORVEXA aims to provide a simple and enjoyable gaming experience while being transparent about the technologies and information used by the app.
